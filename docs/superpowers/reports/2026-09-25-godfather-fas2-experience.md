## 2. What was built

A minimal extension. No parallel memory system.

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| `book.py` | The single loader for the closed-trade book, shared by the pipeline, supervisor and builder. Flags `features_valid` (feature timestamp ≤ entry) and `live`. |
| `experience_builder.py` | Per-trade profile: time to first favourable move / target / SL, levels reached, giveback, management capture, entry success, forecast/thesis errors, observed counterfactual deltas. It then backfills and restates Experience Memory. |
| `experience.py` (extended) | Each pattern now stores `profile` (MFE/MAE quantiles, time-to-event, level reach, giveback, exit mix, calibration, counterfactual deltas with an INSUFFICIENT_DATA floor), `evidence` (calibration/OOS/live counts and lifts) and `entry_quality` (a separate ENTRY_EDGE / ENTRY_FAILURE / NOISE / INSUFFICIENT_DATA class). It also gets `experience_evidence()`, the lookup GODFATHER consumes. |
| `experience_impact.py` | A controlled replay, as-of memory vs empty memory, for entry and position decisions, plus the report. |
| `features.py` | Two regime features: `volatility_bucket`, `funding_bucket` (screener thresholds). |
| `entry_quality.py` | Optional `experience_evidence`: the history sub-score becomes 0.5 + 0.5 × the confidence-weighted signed evidence. |
| Data hygiene | Zero-size positions no longer produce prediction errors, and the 97 junk rows were removed. |

## 3. What was reused

- Path reconstruction, `compute_path_metrics`, `build_experience_memory`
  and its whole anti-noise contract, and the counterfactual engine.
- The prediction-error rows and the MFE model.
- `supervisor.build_entry_signals` for the impact replay, and the
  pipeline's 15-minute sweep, which now uses the same enriched samples, so
  the backfill is never overwritten by a poorer version.

## 4. How the history was backfilled

`load_book` covers 146 closed positions. Of these, 96 are usable: 41
zero-size and 9 with unknown P/L are UNAVAILABLE. Each usable trade gets
a profile from its Guardian path plus the rows already stored in the
database. No exchange fetch and no LLM were used.

Memory is restated in the same sweep. The first 70 % (by close time) is
calibration and the rest is OOS. Every pattern also keeps its own 70/30
walk-forward and its two halves.

## 6. Most robust findings

**None is an edge or a failure pattern.** Every pattern is NOISE (22) or
INSUFFICIENT_DATA (25). For entry quality: NOISE (3) and
INSUFFICIENT_DATA (44).

Descriptive observations, not decision rules:

- Trades that end at SL almost never went favourable first: median MFE
  +0.06 %.
- Target trades have a median MAE of only −0.30 %.
- Forecast calibration error is 0.68 in every BTC regime. The forecast
  role puts about 32 % of its probability mass on what actually happens,
  independent of regime.
- Entry versus management, over the 33 trades where it is observable:
  7 good entries lost money, and 4 bad entries were rescued by the exit.

## 7. Still INSUFFICIENT_DATA

- 25 of 47 P/L patterns and 44 of 47 entry-quality classes.
- Every BTC-bad / BTC-weak comparison (n = 11 each).
- The MFE model at +2 % and +3 %.

**Data gap:** Guardian's first tick comes a median of 27 minutes after
entry (p90: 46 min). "+1 % or −1 % first?" is therefore unobservable for
36 of the 69 trades that moved at least 1 % in some direction: 22 were
decided before the first tick and 14 inside a gap. Reconstructing the
first minutes would require exchange klines. That is the one place where
a fetch could genuinely add information. It was not done in this phase.

## 8. How experience affects GODFATHER's evidence and decisions

- Every advisory entry verdict (159 rows in `godfather_entry_quality`) now
  carries `experience`: matching patterns with n / class / confidence,
  the signed weight and the similar-case profile.
- Only classified patterns carry weight. NOISE and INSUFFICIENT_DATA have
  confidence 0: that is recognition ("I have seen 61 of these"), not
  evidence.
- Measured over the history, **0 of 159 entry decisions and 0 of 77
  position outcomes change**. That is the correct result when nothing is
  classified. The tests prove the consumption works when it should: a
  FAILURE_PATTERN turns a signal into REJECT, an EDGE raises the score,
  and NOISE leaves it identical.
- **Guardian Authority does not read the memory.** Wiring it in would
  change the LIVE safety authority, which this phase forbids. The
  evidence is available in GODFATHER's own advisory layer.
